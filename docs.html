OVERVIEW

What is Sapphire Client?

Sapphire Client is a free Minecraft client for Fabric, Forge and NeoForge. It ships with 35+ built-in mods, HUD elements, quality-of-life mechanics and performance mods, all configurable in game or through the launcher.

Sapphire Client is currently in beta. Features are still being built and improved. Join the Discord to follow updates and report issues.
  • 35+ built-in mods, each one can be toggled and configured
  • A launcher with profile management for Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Minecraft 1.8.9, 1.21 through 1.21.11 and 26.1.2 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge

Getting Started

Follow these simple steps to install Sapphire Client and jump into the game.

Download Launcher

Click the download button at the top right and save the installer to your computer.

Install & Login

Run the installer, open the launcher, and log in with your Minecraft account.

Launch Game

Select your preferred version (1.8.9 or 1.21) and click Play to start the game.
